01 / ROLE OVERVIEW

The Experience

During my internship as a UI/UX Design Intern at PT Cahaya Anugrah Firdaus in Bandung, I contributed to the design process for a legal technology application across desktop and mobile platforms. My work included developing Figma prototypes, creating UML diagrams and flowcharts, supporting usability testing, and preparing Software Requirements Specification (SRS) documentation. These activities helped me understand how system requirements, process logic, and user interactions can be represented through structured design and documentation.
