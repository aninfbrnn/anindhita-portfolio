ILLUSTRATIVE PROCESS DIAGRAM · ACADEMIC ANALYSIS

01 / OVERVIEW

Understanding the user and transaction flow

This academic project examined selected user-facing processes on a digital-asset platform, focusing on account registration, verification, and basic trading workflows.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

From registration to trading

The process analysis covered registration, email and phone verification, PIN setup, and the differences between Limit Order and Market Order workflows.

03 / MY APPROACH

Organizing the process steps

I documented the sequence of user activities and distinguished the main steps in account setup from the actions involved in placing different types of orders.

04 / FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS

Representing key workflow differences

The analysis organized the selected processes into structured descriptions to clarify user actions and the sequence of steps involved.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Business process documentation

I gathered and organized project information and documented the selected workflows as part of an academic assignment.
