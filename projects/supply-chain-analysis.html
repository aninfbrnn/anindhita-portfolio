REPORTED PROCESS · INTERVIEW SUMMARY

01 / OVERVIEW

Understanding the operating flow

This academic project examined how Company X handled stock monitoring and replenishment. Information was collected through an interview with an assistant manager to understand the sequence of activities and the operational details reported during the discussion.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

From stock checks to delivery

The reported workflow included checking warehouse stock, placing orders through online or offline methods, receiving goods, handling payment, and updating inventory records.

03 / MY APPROACH

Mapping the process and handoffs

I documented the sequence of activities and the relationships between stock checks, ordering, goods receipt, payment, and inventory updates.

04 / FINDINGS

Inventory updates inform replenishment

The analysis highlighted how stock monitoring and inventory updates inform replenishment and receipt activities. Potential benefits of more structured information flow were discussed as opportunities for improvement, rather than measured outcomes.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Interview research and process analysis

I collected interview information and analyzed the reported supply chain workflow as part of the Supply Chain Management course project.
