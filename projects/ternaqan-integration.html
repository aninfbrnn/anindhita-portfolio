TERNAQAN · service integration · SUMMARY OF TEAM REPORT

01 / OVERVIEW

Project context

TERNAQAN organizes livestock business information through separate services and GraphQL endpoints. The team report documents service communication, Docker-based deployment, partner-service integration, system architecture, and a web interface.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Individual contribution

The report identifies the team authors but does not allocate individual implementation tasks. Technical outcomes on this page are presented as team deliverables.

03 / KEY ACTIVITIES

Analysis, planning, and delivery

  • Documented Admin, Sapi, Peternak, and Transaction service responsibilities.
  • Defined GraphQL schemas, queries, mutations, and resolvers for service data.
  • Containerized services with Docker and documented deployment and verification.
  • Described partner-service communication and system architecture.
