ILLUSTRATIVE PROCESS DIAGRAM BASED ON PROJECT SUMMARY

01 / OVERVIEW

Understanding the current process

This academic project examined the financial recordkeeping practices and operational challenges at CV. XYZ. Information was gathered through an interview with the business owner, focusing on manual transaction recording, reporting needs, and inventory-management activities.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

From manual records to structured reporting

The project explored how transaction records, financial reports, and inventory information were managed and what challenges arose from the existing approach.

03 / MY APPROACH

Organizing the requirements

I gathered information about the existing workflow, identified reporting and inventory-monitoring needs, and explored how Microsoft Excel could support more structured financial records.

04 / FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS

A more structured financial workflow

The proposed direction included automated trial-balance reporting and inventory forecasting to support financial monitoring and decision-making.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Interview research and system analysis

I collected project information, analyzed the financial-management workflow, and developed recommendations for structured reporting and inventory monitoring.
