Project context

CreditBlock explores a blockchain-based credit lending information system with smart-contract concepts. The report documents user and admin workflows, Scrum sprint activities, and functional and automated tests.

The paper lists group authors but does not allocate individual development or testing tasks. Application features and test artifacts below are team deliverables.

  • Worked through Scrum sprint planning, daily Scrum, review, and retrospective; documented test scenarios.
