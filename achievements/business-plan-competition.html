01 / RECOGNITION

Third Place - National Student Business Plan Competition

Our team earned third place in the national student Business Plan Competition at the 2023 Accounting Information System Competition (AISCOM). The award recognizes a collaborative business-plan entry evaluated in a national student competition setting.

02 / CONTEXT

Student business plan competition

AISCOM 2023 brought together student teams to present business ideas through the lens of accounting information systems. Working toward a competition entry called for turning an opportunity into a coherent plan, communicating its value, and presenting the proposal clearly to evaluators. The result-third place nationally-marks both the team's achievement and a practical opportunity to apply business-planning and presentation skills.
